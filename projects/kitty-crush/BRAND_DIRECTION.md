# WHISKERFOLK — Brand Direction v0.1

## Working brand
**WHISKERFOLK**

Status: strong working title; not yet trademark-cleared.

## Why this name
- Cat-specific without saying "cat" generically.
- Implies a family/community of distinct characters.
- Scales beyond Mochi without losing emotional intimacy.
- Does not anchor the product to match-3.
- Supports IP, character pages, merch and social voice.

## Brand promise
**Every cat has a way of becoming part of your home.**

## Product sentence
A premium cozy mobile game where you find frightened cats, help them through tactile puzzles, earn their trust, bring them home, and discover their personalities as they begin to feel safe.

## Emotional territory
Warmth, rescue, trust, homecoming, quiet humor, tiny surprises, chosen family.

## What WHISKERFOLK is NOT
- shelter-management simulator;
- Tamagotchi obligation loop;
- gacha collector;
- generic match-3 skin;
- hyperactive kids UI;
- sad-animal guilt product.

## Verbal tone
Short, warm, observant, never babyish.

Good:
- “He came back.”
- “Mochi found the warm spot.”
- “Someone moved your blanket.”
- “He’s still deciding if you’re safe.”

Avoid:
- “OMG SO CUTE!!!”
- “Your pet is starving!”
- “Come back NOW or lose your streak.”
- generic fantasy copy.

## Logo direction
A wordmark with one subtle feline behavior cue:
- tail-like terminal stroke;
- paired whisker cuts;
- soft “W” silhouette;
- no paw-print cliché as primary mark.

## App icon direction
Mochi’s face partly emerging from a box opening. Large eyes, asymmetrical ear/mark, warm interior light, no text.
