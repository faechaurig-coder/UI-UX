# WHISKERFOLK — Initial Eight-Cat Roster

Only Mochi is P0 production. Cats 2–8 remain design-ready until Mochi passes attachment validation.

## 1. Mochi — The Cautious Observer
Visual: cream coat, caramel ear/eye comma, moss-green eyes.
Rescue: rainy storefront box.
Favorite object: cardboard box.
Signature: approaches, retreats, looks back, returns.
Relationship lesson: safety is chosen, not forced.

## 2. Churro — The Confident Food Critic
Visual: long orange tabby, darker cinnamon stripes, white chin.
Rescue: bakery loading alley where he has learned delivery schedules.
Favorite object: kitchen mat / puzzle feeder.
Signature: inspects every bowl, pushes the “wrong” one aside.
Personality: social, shameless, opportunistic, dramatic about food.
Relationship lesson: affection can look like showing up every day.

## 3. Nori — The High-Ground Strategist
Visual: near-black coat with blue-green sheen and one white toe.
Rescue: rooftop/service stairwell.
Favorite object: tall shelf / window perch.
Signature: watches from above before dropping silently into the room.
Personality: independent, calm, highly observant.
Relationship lesson: proximity is not the only form of trust.

## 4. Chispa — The Kinetic Calico
Visual: compact calico with one orange “lightning” face patch.
Rescue: mechanic courtyard full of moving objects and hiding spots.
Favorite object: feather wand / rolling spring.
Signature: pounce → skid → pretend it was intentional.
Personality: fearless curiosity, short attention, comic confidence.
Relationship lesson: safety can unlock play.

## 5. Trufa — The Soft Giant
Visual: broad brown tabby, plush cheeks, tiny serious eyes.
Rescue: quiet park storage shed.
Favorite object: oversized cushion.
Signature: slowly occupies an object clearly too small for him.
Personality: patient, tactile, comfort-seeking.
Relationship lesson: calm presence can be deeply expressive.

## 6. Luma — The Sun Collector
Visual: pale silver coat, warm cream chest, amber eyes.
Rescue: greenhouse/service garden.
Favorite object: sun patch / woven mat.
Signature: relocates with the sun throughout the day.
Personality: gentle, routine-loving, easily fascinated by light.
Relationship lesson: home becomes a map of safe places.

## 7. Tinta — The Tiny Engineer
Visual: tuxedo cat with ink-black mask and white “gloves”.
Rescue: back room where she repeatedly opens a loose cabinet.
Favorite object: puzzle box / cabinet latch.
Signature: studies mechanisms, then solves them.
Personality: clever, persistent, mildly mischievous.
Relationship lesson: intelligence itself becomes character.

## 8. Pipa — The Social Bridge
Visual: tortoiseshell with a warm split-color muzzle.
Rescue: community courtyard where several cats tolerate her.
Favorite object: shared blanket.
Signature: inserts herself exactly between two cats and settles.
Personality: socially bold, adaptable, affectionate without clinginess.
Relationship lesson: later cats change earlier cats.

# Roster rules
- No duplicate silhouette.
- No “same cat, different fur”.
- Every cat changes the room behavior graph.
- Every rescue teaches a different kind of trust.
- Each cat must own one screenshot-worthy behavior.
