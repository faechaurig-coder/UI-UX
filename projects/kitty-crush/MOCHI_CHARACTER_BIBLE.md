# MOCHI — Character Bible v0.1

## Role
Mochi is not mascot decoration. Mochi is the proof that WHISKERFOLK works.

## Archetype
**The cautious observer who secretly wants closeness.**

He is not helpless. He evaluates safety.

## Visual identity
- Small young adult / older kitten proportions.
- Cream-white coat with one warm caramel ear and a small caramel “comma” marking beside one eye.
- Slightly oversized ears.
- Round but not chibi-ball body.
- Long expressive tail with darker tip.
- Moss-green eyes, large but grounded.
- One tiny nick on the colored ear: subtle rescue history, not trauma spectacle.

## Silhouette test
Readable through:
- one asymmetrical colored ear;
- tall ear pair;
- low cautious shoulder line;
- long question-mark tail.

## Personality
Core traits:
- cautious;
- observant;
- box-obsessed;
- quietly playful;
- food-motivated but not glutton caricature;
- prefers indirect affection;
- startles easily, recovers through curiosity.

## Likes
- cardboard;
- soft folded fabric;
- sun patches;
- feather movement;
- watching rain from safety;
- sitting near the player without immediately touching.

## Dislikes
- abrupt movement;
- metallic clatter;
- being cornered;
- direct grabbing;
- noisy toy spam.

## Signature behavior
**The double-check.**
Mochi approaches something interesting, retreats two steps, looks back, then returns.

This is his recognizable behavioral fingerprint.

## Signature pose
Half inside a cardboard box with only head + one paw visible.

## Signature sound
A short questioning “mrrp?” more often than a full meow.

## Relationship arc
0 UNKNOWN — only sound/eyes.
1 WATCHING — tracks player.
2 TOLERATING — accepts help at distance.
3 TESTING — approaches/retreats.
4 TRUSTING ACTION — allows rescue.
5 OVERWHELMED — home arrival/hide.
6 SETTLING — chooses first safe spot.
7 INITIATING — first voluntary approach.
8 FAMILIAR — sleeps near player activity.
9 BONDED — seeks player during selected states.

## Body-language vocabulary
### Fear / uncertainty
- ears rotate outward/back;
- body lower;
- tail close to body;
- eye contact broken quickly;
- freeze before retreat.

### Curiosity
- head tilt;
- forward ears;
- slow lean;
- nose twitch;
- single paw step.

### Comfort
- asymmetrical blink;
- shoulders release;
- tail loose;
- loaf/sphinx pose;
- slow eye-close.

### Affection
Never immediate.
- chooses nearby location;
- tail touch;
- cheek rub later;
- sleeps with back exposed;
- voluntary approach.

## Interaction rules
Mochi should not accept every tap.
Low bond:
- player taps near him → looks;
- second rapid tap → backs away;
- slow contextual interaction → remains.

High bond:
- tap near head → lean-in possibility;
- tap on favorite object → shared interaction;
- repeated rapid taps still produce boundary reaction.

## Anti-cliché rules
- no permanent smile;
- no heart particles for every touch;
- no giant anime blush;
- no baby-talk text;
- no “sad because you were away” guilt.
