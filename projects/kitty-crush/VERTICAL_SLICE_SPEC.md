# WHISKERFOLK — Mochi Vertical Slice v0.1

## Target duration
10–15 minutes first-time experience.

## Target emotional curve
curiosity → concern → usefulness → trust → urgency → rescue → quiet relief → belonging

## Scene 1 — RAIN / THE BOX
No logo-first menu.

Black screen.
Rain.
Cardboard scrape.
A small “mrrp?”

Camera reveals a damp alley edge / protected storefront corner.
Mochi's eyes are visible in a box.

Player only receives one contextual prompt:
**Look closer.**

No currency, profile, inbox, pass, streak, shop or event icons.

### Goal
Player identifies a living character before identifying game systems.

## Scene 2 — FIRST HELP / FOOD
Mochi will not exit.

A tactile puzzle appears as the foreground interaction.
Objective: recover 6 food portions from blocked pieces.

On completion:
- no star rating;
- camera returns to world;
- food appears physically;
- Mochi waits, then eats only after player gives distance.

First relationship signal:
**He stayed.**

## Scene 3 — SHELTER
Rain intensifies.
The box roof begins to sag.

Second puzzle introduces:
- blanket pieces;
- soaked cardboard blocker;
- one special-piece tutorial.

Completion visibly changes the box:
- dry cloth cover;
- warmer interior;
- rain sound softens locally.

Mochi performs the first double-check toward the player.

## Scene 4 — TRUST
No puzzle for 20–40 seconds.

Player can:
- hold hand nearby;
- move feather slowly;
- wait.

Correct low-pressure action increases trust.

Rapid tapping causes a mild retreat, not punishment.

Mochi approaches, sniffs, retreats, returns.

## Scene 5 — RESCUE PRESSURE
Water begins entering the corner / street noise grows.
The danger is environmental, not graphic.

Third puzzle:
- clear a safe path;
- drop the carrier latch key;
- protect one “safe tile” from flooding.

This is the first multi-objective puzzle.

## Scene 6 — RESCUE
Mochi stops at the carrier.

Player cannot drag him in.

One quiet interaction:
**Open it and wait.**

Mochi:
- sniffs;
- circles;
- enters voluntarily.

Audio drops to rain + carrier latch.

## Scene 7 — TRANSITION
Short travel montage:
- car/window rain blur;
- carrier breathing;
- no reward summary.

## Scene 8 — THE BOX MOMENT
Warm room.
Carrier/box placed on floor.

Player is told nothing for several seconds.

Mochi:
1. peeks;
2. retreats;
3. peeks again;
4. exits;
5. freezes at room scale;
6. finds the cardboard box left nearby;
7. enters;
8. settles.

Text:
**Mochi lives here now.**

No confetti.

A small Catbook portrait appears only after the moment lands.

## Scene 9 — FIRST HOME FREEDOM
Player can spend 1–2 minutes doing nothing.

Mochi autonomously:
- watches window;
- moves to box;
- sniffs blanket;
- looks toward player.

One object can be placed:
a folded blanket or small scratcher.

Choice produces immediate world change.

## Vertical-slice end
Mochi settles.
A single next-day teaser:
**He hasn't explored the window yet.**

Return to Home, not a level map.

## Acceptance criteria
- 90%+ understand that they rescued Mochi.
- Players can name one Mochi personality trait unaided.
- At least half voluntarily wait/watch during Box Moment without tapping through.
- Players do not describe the first session primarily as “match-3”.
- Home observation without prompt reaches measurable dwell time.
- No screen presents more than one dominant CTA during the first 15 minutes.
